Projects

01

Oxygen Cannula Retention System

Addressed a clinically noted issue in the CHOP Neonatal ICU where ~20% of premature babies inadvertently removed their nasal oxygen cannulas, increasing the risk of hypoxia. Created a displacement stiffness model for cannula tubing attachment and conducted experiments measuring failure, pull-down, and pull-out forces. Defined early-stage design specifications including infant face attachment zone sizing, safe skin-contact parameters, and mobility requirements for use in incubators.

02

Laser Letterboard Communication System

Engineered a sub-$100 head-mounted laser communication device for individuals with ALS, calculating the optimal laser-to-board distance for full board coverage and establishing minimum range of motion requirements for progressive motor decline. Conducted simulation studies across a 5–45° head motion range and collaborated with a multidisciplinary team to produce a formal Design Problem Report (DPR) with defined constraints, test methods, and recommendations.

Assistive TechALS SimulationDPR
Oct – Nov 2025
03

Pediatric Cardiac Wearable Monitor

Investigated the need for child-friendly ambulatory ECG monitoring in toddlers and young children, where pediatric arrhythmias frequently occur beyond the 24–48 hour window of standard Holter devices. Determined key design inputs including patient weight (10–30 kg), device weight (≤20 g), size (5×3×1 cm), and a minimum sampling rate of 250 Hz for accurate QRS detection up to 220 bpm. Collaborated with a design team to translate clinical needs into a comprehensive Design Problem Report.
